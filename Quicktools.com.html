About QuickTools

Last updated: November 2025

QuickTools is a free, privacy-first collection of browser-based utilities for images, text, and calculations. Every tool runs entirely on your device — your files never leave your computer.

Our Mission

We built QuickTools because most online tools ask you to upload your personal files to a stranger's server — and often want your email address, credit card, or a monthly subscription in exchange. We think basic tools should be simple, fast, and private.

Every tool on QuickTools runs entirely in JavaScript inside your browser. That means your images, text, and documents are processed on your own device and never transmitted over the internet. It's not just a promise — it's how the code works.

What We Offer

🖼️ Image Tools

Upscale to 16K, compress, resize, crop, rotate, enhance, blur, and convert between PNG, JPG, and WebP — with batch processing for 100+ files.

📝 Text Tools

Count words, change case, remove duplicates, generate slugs, encode text, create Lorem Ipsum, and export to PDF.

🧮 Calculators

Age, BMI, loan EMI, percentage, discount, compound interest, date difference, unit and temperature conversion.

🛠️ More Tools

QR code generation and PDF utilities (coming with bundled libraries) alongside our other helpful utilities.

Why Choose QuickTools?

  • 100% private: Files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or stored on a server.
  • Completely free: No accounts, no sign-up, no subscription, no daily limits.
  • Batch processing: Process 50–100 images in a single click and download everything as a ZIP file.
  • Works offline: Once the page has loaded, the tools continue to work without an internet connection.
  • No installation: Everything runs in any modern browser on desktop or mobile.
  • Transparent code: The entire site is client-side JavaScript that anyone can inspect.
How it works: QuickTools uses modern browser APIs like the Canvas API and the File API to read, edit, and export your files locally. When you click "Process", the browser performs all the work in memory on your own device.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information QuickTools collects, what it does not collect, and how we use cookies and third-party services such as advertising.

1. The Short Version

  • All file processing happens inside your browser — your files are never uploaded.
  • We do not require an account and do not ask for personal information to use any tool.
  • We use a small number of cookies for analytics and advertising (via Google AdSense).
  • You can use most tools even with all cookies blocked.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

QuickTools does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to:

  • Images, videos, or any files you process
  • Text you paste into text tools
  • Calculations you perform
  • Your name, email, or any personal identifier (unless you contact us voluntarily via WhatsApp or email)

All file and text data is processed client-side using JavaScript APIs (Canvas, File, Blob) and is discarded from memory when you close the tool or the page.

3. Contact Form Information

When you use our Contact form, the information you provide (name, email, phone number, reason, message, profile picture, and ID card image) is sent directly to our WhatsApp number using your own WhatsApp app. It is not sent to, stored on, or processed by any QuickTools server.
